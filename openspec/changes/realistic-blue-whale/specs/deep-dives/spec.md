## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Surfacing series and deep dives
The whale SHALL surface in a series of 2 to 6 blows, then dive to feed, typically to 100 to 200 m and occasionally to about 300 m, never closer to the seafloor than its clearance; a whole dive SHALL take about four to six minutes at journey pace (a real one eight to fifteen). About three dives in ten SHALL lift the flukes clear of the water. The whale SHALL feed on krill only, in swarms 80 to 200 m down.

#### Scenario: Dive depth
- **WHEN** a feeding dive starts over water deeper than 250 m
- **THEN** the whale reaches at least 100 m and at most 320 m below the surface

#### Scenario: Shallow water
- **WHEN** the floor ahead is shallower than the planned dive plus the clearance
- **THEN** the dive is shallower and the whale never goes below the clearance rail
