Fly

A small ship drifting through a universe that goes on forever. Every star, cloud, planet and stone is procedurally generated on your device from one number, the seed; the same seed opens the same universe for anyone.

It flies itself. Leave it open beside your work and glance over now and then.

Drag looks around, right-drag steers, the wheel zooms, arrow keys nudge, space pauses. Drag steers, two fingers look around, pinch zooms.

Nothing is collected: no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sent anywhere. Your sound settings and where you were are kept only in this browser.
