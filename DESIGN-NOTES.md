# calm logo directions

Three directions in `logos/<direction>/` (mark, mark-ink, mark-paper, logo-primary, logo-paper, favicon).
Everything is generated by `tools/logo/build.py` (ribbon geometry + "calm" outlined from Manrope-Variable at wght 600 via fonttools)
and `tools/logo/previews.py` (resvg-js PNGs, contact sheet). Previews are in `previews/`.

Shared system (matches the ejm-ui product family): 256×256 mark viewBox, filled ribbons of ~36-unit weight with fully rounded
caps and one-sided taper, at most one accent shape, palette limited to Sage #8A9A7B / Ink #202721 / Paper #F6F5EF / Forest #29443B
(no blue). Wordmark is Manrope 600, lowercase, size 200 on a 256 artboard, 30 units right of the mark. Primary logo = Sage mark +
Ink wordmark; paper logo = all Paper. Favicon = Paper mark at 76% on a square Forest tile, with weights bumped to 44-46 for small sizes.
No raster, scripts, external refs, filters, gradients or `<text>`.

## A. breach
**Idea:** a low humpback back (long head slope, steeper tail side) with a swept-back dorsal fin, riding over a waterline pill. The fin
is the whale cue; without it the arc read as a dome/bell. **Tokens:** Sage mark, 36-unit ribbon tapering to 24 at the tail, fin
ribbon 32 to 14, round caps. **Small sizes:** the fin merges into the arc at 16 px and the favicon reads as a dome with a nick;
this is the weakest direction at favicon size. Whale cue is clearer large than small.

## B. fluke
**Idea:** two broad, swept-back lobes (a leaf-shaped width profile, 40 to 50 to 12) meet at a shallow notch, then taper into a
peduncle that lands on the waterline pill. It no longer has the horn/Ψ look. **Tokens:** two mirrored tapered ribbons, a
34-to-22 peduncle ribbon, a 28-unit bar, round caps. **Small sizes:** the favicon drops the bar and widens the weights; the lobes
plus stem still read as a whale tail at 16/32 px. The notch is subtle and the lobes are heavier than cli/ide.

## C. swell
**Idea:** the dot is gone, which removes the swimmer pictogram. Two nested ribbons curl like a rolling wave, in the manner of the
ejmlabs symbol: a large crest that curls over, and a shorter ribbon tucked underneath it with a clear gap. **Tokens:** 36-unit ribbons
tapering to 26/28, round caps, Sage/Ink/Paper. **Small sizes:** two shapes with a wide gap; the favicon uses 41-unit weights and the
curl stays legible at 16 px. It reads as a wave or fiddlehead, not as a whale; the nesting is a little tight at the curl.

## Known weaknesses
- breach's favicon is busy at 16 px; consider dropping the fin from the favicon only.
- swell may read as a fiddlehead or ear at a glance.
- Wordmark baseline is a fixed y=190 per direction; no kerning applied.
- Not wired into the site; no ico/png asset set beyond favicon previews.

# fluke v2 (SUPERSEDED by v3 below; kept for history; chosen direction, "Fluke, but more polish")

Files: `logos/fluke/{mark-sage,mark-ink,mark-paper,logo-primary,logo-ink,logo-paper,icon-forest,icon-sage,favicon}.svg`.
Generated by `tools/logo/build.py` (builds only fluke now; breach/swell files are untouched) using `tools/logo/fluke.py`;
`tools/logo/previews.py` renders `previews/fluke-{light,dark}.png`, `fluke-favicon-{16,32,48}.png`, `fluke-icon-*.png`, `contact-sheet.png`.

**What changed from v1.** v1 was two filled leaf blades on a floating stem over a pill, which read as a goblet. v2: each lobe is a
swelling ribbon (42 units at the root, tapering to an 8-unit round tip) whose centreline sweeps up and out along a convex leading
edge and flicks up at the tip. The two lobes cross in a shallow V notch. The peduncle is a single ribbon that flows out of the
lobes and runs on below them. Two short 26-unit ribbons (round caps, family accent) sit either side of it as the water surface,
so the tail reads as rising *through* the water; there is no base to read as a pedestal. The mark is centred on the 256 grid by
measured bounding box and its ink spans ~236 wide, so it has the same footprint as siblings. Wordmark: Manrope 600 outlined,
size 216, baseline 205, `x0=268` (about one mark-width plus a ~22-unit gap, as ide). Titles follow "calm by ejmlabs, <variant>".

**Explorations** (`previews/fluke-explorations/`, a/b/c at 512 px plus 16/32 px favicons):
- **a (chosen)** tail passing through two ripple ribbons. Clearest, no pedestal reading, best at 16/32 px.
- **b** full-width waterline ribbon under a short stem with a gap. The stem+bar still suggests a goblet/pedestal.
- **c** no base: peduncle curves into a hook-shaped wave tail. Elegant, but asymmetric, and at 16 px the hook reads as a stray mark.
Earlier internal rounds (not kept): smile-shaped lobes with upturned tips looked like bull horns; the fix was a convex, gull-wing
leading edge so tips sweep outward rather than curling up, which is what makes it a humpback tail.

**Favicon.** Sage tile with Ink mark (matching the family's live favicons), mark at 92% of the tile, a simplified version with
no ripples, weights x1.1 and a longer stem. It fills the tile width; height is ~55% because a fluke is inherently wide, up from ~40%.
At 16 px it reads as a Y-shaped tail with a dark stem; at 32 px the notch and tip flicks are visible.
`icon-forest`/`icon-sage` use the full mark at 76% like the family icons.

**Known weaknesses.** The lobes are still heavier than cli/ide ribbons (the family's ribbons do not swell). Some will read
the 16 px favicon as a "Y". Marks are ~4.4 KB (dense flattened paths, as in the family's generated SVGs), slightly over the 4 KB goal.

# fluke v3 (current; response to REVIEW-FLUKE.md)

v2 read as a longhorn skull (deep V crotch, thin upturned tapering tips, stem narrowing to a muzzle) and as a "Y" at 16 px.
v2's renders are kept as `previews/fluke-explorations/v2-longhorn-mark.svg` and `v2-contact-sheet.png`.

**Rebuild.** `tools/logo/fluke.py` now draws one hand-authored outline (left half authored, right half mirrored) instead of swelling ribbons:
- Broad, near-level blade: the lobes meet at y≈142 and the tips are only ~25 units higher.
- The notch is a small rounded dip (9 units deep, ~16 wide), not an edge crossing.
- The trailing edge runs out in a gentle scallop to round tips swept slightly up (no points, no horn flick).
- The leading edge returns in one convex sweep and fillets into the peduncle; the stem is 36 wide and flares into the blade,
  so it grows out of the tail (no concave corners, no muzzle). Visible stem below the blade is ~50 units.
- Ripples are 30-unit pills (family weight), 56 long, with a ≥20-unit gap to the stem and set low on the stem.
- Mark is ~232 wide with ≥12 units of side clearance, centred vertically by measured bbox. The lockup gap is ~55 units
  (wordmark x0=284), between ide's 40 and relay's 56. Marks are now ~0.75 KB, logos ~2.7 KB.
- Favicon: Sage tile, Ink mark at 92%, no ripples, rounder tips (r12), 40-wide stem, 10-unit notch. At 16 px it is a broad
  tail silhouette, not a Y; the notch and stem still read at 32 px (see the 8x/4x/3x blow-ups in the contact sheet).
- The lobes are no longer swelling ribbons; this is a calm-specific, literal-fluke exception to the family's constant-weight
  ribbon rule and shouldn't be copied by other products. The fallback (two open curled ribbon lobes) was not needed.

**Remaining risks.** The two ripple pills can read as eyes/"ears" beside the stem in the lockup; if that bites, use a single ripple
ribbon broken by the stem (relay-like). The icons carry the ripples at 76% and are busy below ~192 px; use favicon.svg there.

**v3.1 (final polish).** Per Pass 2 of REVIEW-FLUKE.md: the ripple pills moved 10 units lower (now flush with the stem end, clearance to the stem unchanged) so they no longer sit in the blade's shadow and read as a face; the peduncle now has vertical flanks ending in a true semicircle (r18 on the mark, r20 on the favicon) instead of a flared, slightly bulbous end; and the leading edge was pulled up ~4 units mid-span to thin the blade, which lightens it toward the family without raising the trailing edge, so no horn reading returns. Nothing else in the concept changed.
